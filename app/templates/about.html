    Pengo

    We make trip planning
    feel like the easy part

    Pengo is a travel planning platform that turns a destination search into a ready-to-go, personalised itinerary — in under a minute, for anywhere in the world.

    Why we built Pengo

    Planning a trip used to mean hours of research — scrolling through hundreds of attraction listings, reading conflicting reviews, mapping out routes manually, and still wondering if you'd picked the right places.

    We built Pengo to eliminate that. Tell us your travel style once. Search any city. Get a personalised, route-optimised day plan built around you — not around what's most popular for everyone else.

    Every recommendation is backed by real ratings and live data from millions of verified travellers, matched to your budget, your interests, and your preferred travel pace.

    What We Believe

    Our principles

    The decisions that shape every feature we build.

    You first, always

    Every recommendation is personalised to your budget, interests, and travel style — not to generic popularity charts that look the same for everyone.

    Real data, real reviews

    We only surface attractions with live ratings from verified travellers worldwide. No sponsored placements, no inflated scores.

    Efficient routes, by design

    Your day plan is ordered to minimise commute time between stops. More exploring, less navigating.

    Gets smarter over time

    Every trip you rate improves your future recommendations. The more you use Pengo, the more it understands what you love.

    Anywhere on earth

    No curated city list. Search any destination worldwide and Pengo fetches live attraction data on the spot.

    Private by default

    Your travel data stays yours. We never share your profile or preferences with third parties. Your API calls are server-side — keys never leave our backend.
